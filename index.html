📜 6 Official UPI Cyber Safety Rules

Cybersecurity Mantra: Safe UPI, Safe India (Aligned with RBI Kehta Hai & NPCI Guidelines)

Rule 01
The Golden PIN Rule

UPI PIN is ONLY entered to DEDUCT money from your account. You NEVER need to enter your PIN to receive money, prizes, or refunds!

Rule 02
The QR Code Reality

QR codes work in ONE direction only: to SEND money. Scanning a QR code sent to you on WhatsApp will drain your balance, not credit it.

Rule 03
Zero Screen-Sharing

Never install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, RustDesk, or QuickSupport on instructions from any caller. They can view your screen and steal OTPs.

Rule 04
No "Digital Arrest" in Law

Indian police and CBI NEVER place citizens under "Digital Arrest" over Skype or WhatsApp video call, nor do they demand money on UPI.

Rule 05
Decline Unknown Collect Requests

If your UPI app pops up asking you to "Pay ₹5,000" to receive cashback or prize money, immediately click DECLINE.

Rule 06
Golden Golden Hour (1930)

If defrauded, call 1930 within 2 to 3 hours ("The Golden Hour") to freeze the stolen funds before the scammer can cash them out.

🛡️ Official UPI Safety Handbook

Remember these 4 Key Principles for UPI Cyber Safety:

  1. UPI PIN is a Debit Key: Never enter it to accept payments.
  2. QR Code = Sending Money: You only need to share your mobile number or UPI ID (e.g. name@okhdfcbank) to receive money.
  3. Remote Desktop = Device Hijack: Customer care will never ask you to install AnyDesk or TeamViewer.
  4. Government Agencies Don't Call on WhatsApp Video: Police, CBI, and RBI never ask for payments over UPI.
🚨 Emergency 1930 Helpline & Complaint Guide
ACT FAST! The first 2 hours are known as "The Golden Hour".
Calling 1930 promptly gives cyber police the best chance of freezing the fraudster's beneficiary bank account.
Step-by-Step Reporting Procedure:
  1. Dial 1930: Call from your registered mobile phone.
  2. Provide Details: Note down your bank name, account number, transaction UTR number (12 digits), and date/time.
  3. Acknowledgement SMS: You will receive a formal incident complaint ID.
  4. File on Portal: Visit cybercrime.gov.in to upload transaction screenshots.
